Charades! privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Charades! is a party game for iPhone by Touch Media OÜ. The game does not collect any data about you and does not send data to us or to anyone else.

No account, ads or tracking

You do not need to sign up or enter anything about yourself to play. The game has no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Game video

When game video is turned on, the camera and microphone record the people acting out the words during a round: the front camera when the phone is on the forehead, the back camera in TV mode. The video is made and kept only on your phone. It is added to your photo library only when you tap the save button, and shared only when you share it yourself. If you do not save it, it is deleted. You can turn game video off in the game's settings and remove camera and microphone access in iPhone Settings.

Tilting the phone

The phone's motion sensors are used only to tell whether you tilted the phone down or up. This data is not stored.

Your decks and settings

Your own decks, the words you have played and your settings are kept only on your phone.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; no notifications are sent from our servers. You can turn them off in the game's settings.

TV mode

The TV connects to the phone over AirPlay or a cable. What the TV shows, including the camera picture while filming, goes straight from the phone to the TV and not through our servers.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple. We do not see your payment details or Apple ID. Apple's handling of data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, so none from children either.

Changes and contact

If the game ever uses data differently, we will update this page before it does. Questions: Touch Media OÜ, oytouchmedia@gmail.com.
